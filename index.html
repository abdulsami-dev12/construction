Details that matter

Why choose a considered approach?

The best results are built from small decisions made at the right time.

Clear scope

Keep drawings, responsibilities and
exclusions together so everyone can work
from the same information.

Open communication

Agree who makes decisions, when
information is needed and how changes
are recorded.

Practical detailing

Think about access, maintenance,
junctions and how materials will perform
in everyday use.

Realistic sequencing

Allow time for surveys, approvals,
procurement and work that needs to
happen in order.

A cared-for site

Discuss site access, separation from
occupied areas and the daily routines
that protect people

A useful handover

Collect operating information, finish
schedules and a clear route for reporting
outstanding items.
